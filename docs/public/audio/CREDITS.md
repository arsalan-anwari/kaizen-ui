# Audio credits

The five kana clips in this folder (`a`, `ka`, `shi`, `tsu`, `n`) come from the
[Kana Sounds](https://huggingface.co/datasets/arsalan-anwari/kana-sounds) dataset,
recorded by [FUN Japanese Learning](https://funjapaneselearning.com) and used under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

They are demo assets for the docs site only; they are not part of the published
`kaizen-ui` package.
